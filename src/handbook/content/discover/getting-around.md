## Sign in

- **Preferred:** Google Sign-In with your approved Workspace or Gmail account (must already exist in People).
- **Fallback:** Password sign-in on the login page when Google is unavailable or not configured.

New accounts are created by an **Admin** — Google Sign-In does not auto-create users.

## Navigate the Hub

The left sidebar groups modules:

- **Overview** — Dashboard, Hub Handbook, Management Overview (if you have access), Support Updates, Reported Issues
- **Quality & performance** — KPI, QA, Negative Reviews, Coaching
- **Knowledge & product** — Product Guides, Integration Steps, Supported Files, Token Migration, Product Feedback, Feature Impact, Knowledge Base

Use the account menu for profile, password, theme, and (for Admins) User management. **View As** shows another user’s read-only experience — mutations are blocked while active.

## Your dashboard

What you see depends on role:

| Experience | Typical roles |
| --- | --- |
| **Classic dashboard** | Agent, Tech Agent — greeting, attention, updates, your KPI strip (Agents), module tasks |
| **Operations workspace** | Supervisor, Senior Agent, Admin — team responsibilities, integration/QA progress |

Open **Dashboard** at the start of every shift. Work **Needs your attention** before diving into trackers.

## Attention, tasks, and toasts

- **Needs your attention** — personalized queue (coaching, QA, integrations assigned to you, pending guide work, etc.). Not the same as “can open the module.”
- **Module tasks** — dashboard boards per tracker (e.g. files needing review, DSAT check due).
- **Hub notification toasts** — poll in the UI for coaching, integration, @mentions, QA events.
- **Support Updates** — may require acknowledgement from the dashboard; separate from the attention aggregator.
