You joined KoinX support — welcome. **Support Hub** is your internal operations home: KPI, quality, documentation trackers, coaching, and team communication. It is **not** where customer tickets live.

## Why we built the Hub

Before the Hub, critical support knowledge and metrics were scattered across spreadsheets and chat. The Hub gives us:

- **One place** to see what needs your attention today
- **Structured trackers** for guides, integrations, issues, and feedback
- **Fair quality loops** — QA, appeals, coaching — tied to real rubrics
- **Accountability** — who owns KPI entry, DSAT checks, and publication

Weeks start **Monday**; operational dates use **Asia/Kolkata (IST)** unless a screen says otherwise.

## Hub vs Freshdesk (short)

| | Freshdesk | Support Hub |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Purpose** | Customer tickets, SLAs, escalations to customers | Internal metrics, QA, docs, ops issues |
| **When to use** | Every customer conversation | Your shift checklist, team records, internal fixes |
| **Escalations** | Customer escalations **only here** | Hub “escalations” module is **retired** |

> **Tip:** If a customer is waiting on a reply, work in **Freshdesk**. If documentation is wrong or a process is broken, use **Reported Issues** or the relevant tracker in the Hub.

## Who uses the Hub

Five roles — **Agent**, **Senior Agent**, **Supervisor**, **Tech Agent**, and **Admin** — each see different modules in the app. This handbook shows **your** learning path; Supervisors and Admins can preview other roles without changing real permissions.
