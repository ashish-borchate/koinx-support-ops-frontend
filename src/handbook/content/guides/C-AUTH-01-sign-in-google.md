---
title: Sign in with Google
---

## Steps

1. Open the Hub login page at `/login`.
2. Click **Sign in with Google**.
3. Choose your approved work Google account.
4. After success, you land on the **Dashboard**.

## Expected outcome

You are signed in; your name appears in the header. Sidebar modules match your role.

## Common mistakes

- Using a personal Google email that does not match your employee record.
- Expecting auto-provision without an Admin-created user.
