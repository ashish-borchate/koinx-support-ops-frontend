---
title: Enter weekly agent KPI
---

## Steps

1. Open **KPI Performance**.
2. Select the reporting week and agent.
3. Enter tickets handled, FRT, ART, resolution, SLA, and CSAT (survey counts or legacy % per record).
4. Save the record and verify the scorecard preview.

## Expected outcome

Agent week row shows updated metrics with correct sources.

## Common mistakes

- Agents attempting team entry (Supervisor/Admin responsibility).
- Mixing IST week boundaries (week starts Monday IST).
