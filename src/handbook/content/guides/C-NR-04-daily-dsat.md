---
title: Complete Daily DSAT check
---

## Steps

1. Confirm you are the **KPI reporting owner** (Admin KPI settings).
2. Open dashboard module task or `/daily-dsat?date=YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. Choose **DSAT found** or **No DSAT found**.
4. Add an optional note; submit.

## Expected outcome

Date no longer listed as pending; history shows your submission.

## Common mistakes

- Non-owners attempting submit (403).
- Skipping weekend days (Sun excluded; Mon–Sat expected).
