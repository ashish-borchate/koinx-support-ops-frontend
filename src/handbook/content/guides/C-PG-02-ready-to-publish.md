## Steps

1. Open the guide in **Draft** or **Changes Required**.
2. Confirm Mintlify documentation is updated and confirmed in the Hub.
3. Complete the **ready-to-publish** verification checklist.
4. Submit; status becomes **Ready to Publish**.

## Expected outcome

Supervisor publish queue shows the guide; publish SLA timers apply per product rules.

## Common mistakes

- Expecting Senior Agent approval (retired).
