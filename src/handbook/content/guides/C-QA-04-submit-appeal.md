---
title: Submit a QA appeal
---

## Steps

1. Open your QA review detail page.
2. Confirm the appeal window is open (default **7 days**, production unverified).
3. Select criteria to appeal with reasons/evidence.
4. Submit the appeal and monitor status under **QA → Appeals**.

## Expected outcome

Appeal status `submitted` / `under_review`; reviewer notified via Hub workflow.

## Common mistakes

- Submitting after the window closes.
- Appealing on legacy rubric reviews if appeals are disabled.
