---
title: Daily routine
audience: agent
---

## Start of shift

1. Sign in with **Google** (preferred) or password fallback.
2. Open the **Dashboard** — review **Needs your attention** and **module tasks**.
3. Read **Support Updates**; acknowledge if required.
4. Open **Freshdesk** and work your ticket queue.

## During the shift

- Complete **daily product guide review** if it appears on the dashboard.
- Check **QA** for new reviews; acknowledge or appeal within the window (**7-day default**, production unverified).
- Respond to **Coaching** acknowledgements.
- Submit **Product Feedback** when you discover product issues.

## End of shift

- Ensure no Hub acknowledgement is blocking you for the next day.
- Do not enter **team KPI** data—that is Supervisor/Admin work.
