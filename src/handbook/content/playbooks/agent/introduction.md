This playbook is for **Agents**—the people handling customer tickets in Freshdesk and using the Hub for personal performance, quality feedback, and team knowledge.

Permissions in the app may allow you to **view** some team areas (for example, aggregated KPI context). Your **day-to-day responsibilities** below focus on what Agents typically own in the Hub.

## Your responsibilities in the Hub

- **Customer work in Freshdesk** — primary job; the Hub does not replace tickets.
- **Review your QA feedback** — open Quality Assurance when you receive a new evaluation; acknowledge when required.
- **Appeals** — if you disagree with a scored QA review on the current rubric, you may submit an appeal within the appeal window. Code defaults are **7 calendar days** to appeal and **3 calendar days** to escalate after a reviewer decision; confirm with your lead if your environment uses different settings (production values are not verified in this handbook).
- **Coaching** — complete acknowledgements when coaching is assigned to you.
- **Product guide daily review** — when the dashboard shows a daily review task, complete it under Product Guides.
- **Product feedback** — submit observations via Product Feedback when you discover issues or ideas.
- **Reported issues** — report operational or documentation problems (including from contextual “Report issue” actions).
- **Support Updates** — read updates; acknowledge when your team requires it.
- **Negative reviews** — acknowledge coaching on your own negative review records when prompted.

You **do not** enter team KPI records—that is primarily **Supervisor** work (Admins can as well).

## A simple daily routine

1. Sign in with **Google** (or password fallback).
2. Open the **Dashboard** — address attention items.
3. Work tickets in **Freshdesk**.
4. Check **QA** and **Coaching** for anything awaiting acknowledgement.
5. Complete any **daily guide review** or Support Update acknowledgement shown.

## Common mistakes

- Trying to escalate **customers** only in the Hub — use **Freshdesk**.
- Assuming you can edit **team KPI** data — that belongs to Supervisors/Admins.
- Missing an **appeal deadline** — watch dates on the QA detail page.
