## QA acknowledgement and appeals

Open **Quality Assurance** → your review → acknowledge when required. To dispute rubric scores, use **Submit appeal** while the window is open.

## Coaching

Open **Coaching Center** when assigned; complete acknowledgement or follow your lead's instructions for disputes.

## Report an issue

Use **Report issue** on a guide/integration or create a general issue under **Reported Issues**.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Instead |
| --- | --- |
| Escalating customers only in the Hub | Escalate in **Freshdesk** |
| Editing team KPI | Ask your **Supervisor** |
| Missing appeal deadline | Calendar the deadline from the QA page |
