Senior Agents lead **quality** and selected **operational reviews** while staying hands-on in the Hub.

## Core responsibilities

- **Create and edit Agent QA** evaluations (`qa.create`).
- **Review integration steps** when assigned.
- Manage **negative reviews** records with Supervisors' operational model.
- **Daily DSAT checks** when you are the configured **KPI reporting owner** (Admin sets this in KPI settings; ops intends Harsh when assigned).

## What is retired

- **Senior Agent product guide approve/request-changes** — Supervisor-driven publishing only.
- **Agent QA supervisor validation** — retired API.

## Modules you use often

QA, Negative Reviews, Integration Steps (review), Coaching, Management Overview (if enabled), Product Feedback (view/submit).
