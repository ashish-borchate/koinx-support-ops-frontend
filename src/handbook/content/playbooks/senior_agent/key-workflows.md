## Integration review

Open **Integration Steps** from attention or tracker → review assigned records → request changes or approve per your permissions.

## Negative reviews

Log and categorize reviews; link **coaching** when required.

## Appeals

Use **QA → Appeals** to process reviewer decisions; escalate per policy when agents escalate within the **3-day default** window (production unverified).

## Coaching

Create or update coaching records when you own quality follow-up (with Supervisor alignment).
