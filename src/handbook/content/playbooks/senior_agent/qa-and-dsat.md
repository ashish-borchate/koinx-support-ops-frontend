## Agent QA

1. Open **Quality Assurance** → **New review**.
2. Select an **Agent** subject and ticket context.
3. Score criteria on the **KoinX rubric**; save the review.
4. Respond to **appeals** when you are the assigned reviewer.
5. When a Supervisor flags a **Random QA audit** on your evaluation, **Acknowledge** or **Disagree** with their feedback from the QA record or your dashboard tasks. This is coaching on your review quality—it does **not** change the Agent’s score.

Supervisors may record **Senior Agent QA** on supervisor-handled work via a separate QA mode—that is not Agent QA.

## Daily DSAT

If you are the **reporting owner**:

1. Check dashboard **module tasks** for pending IST dates (Mon–Sat).
2. Open `/daily-dsat` for each date.
3. Record **DSAT found** or **No DSAT found** with notes if needed.

If you do not see tasks, ask an **Admin** to confirm KPI settings → **Reporting** tab (owner + launch date).

> **Important:** Production reporting owner is **unverified** in documentation; defaults in code do not replace Admin configuration.
