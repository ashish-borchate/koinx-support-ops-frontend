Supervisors run **day-to-day support operations** in the Hub: people below Admin, KPI ownership, publishing, triage, and coaching management.

## Primary KPI ownership

You are the **main owner** of entering and updating **agent KPI** records. Admins can also edit and configure goals.

## Other core areas

- **Product Guides** publish and accuracy review (not Senior Agent approval).
- **Product Feedback** approve/reject.
- **Reported Issues** triage and assignment.
- **Coaching** manage queue.
- **Integration Steps** manage/publish.
- **Negative reviews** and team quality visibility.

## Workspace

You typically see the **Operations Workspace** dashboard with team context and module tasks.
