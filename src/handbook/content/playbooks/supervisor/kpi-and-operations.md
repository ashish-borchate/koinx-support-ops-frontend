## Weekly KPI entry

1. Open **KPI Performance** after the reporting week (IST Monday boundary).
2. Select each agent and enter FRT, ART, resolution, SLA, CSAT (counts or % per your process), and ensure QA linkage is correct.
3. Review scorecard breakdowns with agents in 1:1s as needed.

Admins configure **targets and weights**; you do not need `kpi.manage_goals` for routine entry.

## Dashboard operations

- Clear **Needs your attention** (publish queues, PF pending, coaching unassigned, etc.).
- Work **module tasks** boards before they go overdue.
- Use **Management Overview** for period snapshots when reviewing team health.

## Reported Issues

Triage **Pending Review** on resource-linked issues; assign owners; move to **In Progress** / **Resolved** / **No Action Required**.
