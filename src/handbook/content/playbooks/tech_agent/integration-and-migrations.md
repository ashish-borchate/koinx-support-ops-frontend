## Integration frontend work

1. Open **Integration Steps** from dashboard attention or tracker.
2. Open the assigned integration record.
3. Complete **Pending Frontend** work; follow team standards for handoff.

## Token migration acknowledgement

1. Open **Token Migration** tracker.
2. Find records marked **informed** and not yet acknowledged.
3. Acknowledge when your team has completed the technical action.

## Common mistakes

- Trying to publish integrations without **manage/publish** permissions—coordinate with a Supervisor.
- Ignoring dashboard attention items— they reflect assignments to you specifically.
