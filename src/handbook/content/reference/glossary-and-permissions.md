title: Glossary & who owns what
description: Terms and operational ownership for support employees.
---

The Hub uses five roles: **Admin**, **Supervisor**, **Senior Agent**, **Agent**, and **Tech Agent**.

## Capability vs responsibility

| Idea | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **What you can open** | Pages and actions the Hub allows for your role. |
| **What you own** | What your job expects you to do — sometimes narrower, sometimes broader than what you can open. |

Examples:

- **Supervisors** own **weekly KPI entry** for agents. Agents view their own scorecard.
- **Senior Agents** create **Agent QA** evaluations. Daily **DSAT** is done only by the **reporting owner** set by an Admin — not every Senior Agent automatically.
- **Tech Agents** acknowledge **token migrations** and complete integration **frontend** tasks.
- **Admins** configure people, KPI targets, reporting owner, and QA appeal windows; they do not replace Supervisors on the floor every day.

## Attention vs access

You may open a module but not see every open item on your dashboard. **Needs your attention** and **module tasks** follow assignment and role rules.

## View As

Leads may preview another employee’s Hub in read-only mode. It does not change what that person can do when they sign in themselves.
