# Troubleshooting & FAQs

## I cannot sign in

- Use your approved Google account or password fallback if your admin enabled it.
- Accounts are invite-only — Google Sign-In does not create new users.

## I do not see Daily DSAT tasks

Active **Senior Agents** see Daily DSAT tasks after an Admin sets a **DSAT launch date** under KPI Performance → Settings → Reporting. The task is role-based, not tied to a named reporting owner.

## My QA appeal button is missing

- Appeals are only for **KoinX rubric** Agent QA reviews you own.
- The appeal window is a fixed number of calendar days from review creation (see live Admin handbook settings or your review detail).
- If you already appealed or the window closed, the Hub blocks a new appeal.

## DSAT / KPI numbers look wrong

- Weekly KPI entry is manual — Supervisors (and Admins) enter metrics; Agents see their own scorecard.
- QA on scorecards uses the **effective** score after closed appeals.

## View As

View As is read-only — you cannot submit DSAT checks, appeals, or edits while previewing another user.
