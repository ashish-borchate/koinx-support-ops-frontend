Three different surfaces help you prioritize work. They are **not** interchangeable.

## Needs your attention

Personalized items built from live records in trackers (assignments, publish queues, mentions, acknowledgements). Broad module access does **not** mean every open item appears here—only what you are expected to act on.

## Module tasks (dashboard)

Kanban-style boards grouped by module and stage (for example Product Guides ready to publish, Daily DSAT dates, integration review). Open the board from your dashboard when you have pending tasks.

## Hub notification toasts

Short prompts for coaching, integration updates, @mentions, and some QA workflow events. These poll in the background while you work.

## Support Updates acknowledgements

Required acknowledgements may appear on the dashboard feed. They are **not** mixed into the same rules as workflow attention.

## Email and Slack

The Hub does **not** send operational email or Slack notifications today. Check the dashboard when you start your shift.

| Signal | Where to look |
| --- | --- |
| Assigned reported issue | Attention + Reported Issues |
| Product guide publish queue | Attention (Supervisor role rules) |
| Daily DSAT | Module tasks → `/daily-dsat` (reporting owner only) |
| @mention on a comment | Toast + opening the record marks read |
