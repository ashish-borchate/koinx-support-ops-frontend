---
title: Daily operational practices
description: Healthy habits for every shift.
audience: all
---

1. **Freshdesk first** for live customers; Hub for internal quality and documentation.
2. Open the **Dashboard** and clear or progress **attention** and **module tasks**.
3. Read **Support Updates**; acknowledge when required.
4. Supervisors: keep **KPI** records current for the active reporting week.
5. Senior Agents (when assigned): complete **Daily DSAT** for all due IST dates (Mon–Sat).
6. Agents: check **QA** and **Coaching** for acknowledgements or open appeals before deadlines.
7. Use **Report an issue** on resources instead of only chatting informally when something needs tracking.
8. Prefer **Google Sign-In**; use password fallback only when needed.

Week boundaries for KPI are **Monday 00:00 Asia/Kolkata**.
