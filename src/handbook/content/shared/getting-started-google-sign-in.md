You need an **employee account** before you can use the Hub. An Admin or Supervisor creates your user in **User management**; the Hub does not auto-create accounts when you sign in with Google.

## Preferred sign-in: Google

1. Open the Hub login page.
2. Choose **Sign in with Google**.
3. Use your **approved work Google account** (Workspace or Gmail with a verified email).
4. On first successful Google sign-in, the Hub links your Google identity to your employee record.

If Google Sign-In is not configured in an environment, the button may be unavailable—use password fallback or contact an Admin.

### Common Google sign-in issues

- **“No active employee account exists for this email”** — Your email is not in User management yet, or the account is inactive. Contact an Admin.
- **“This Google account does not match the employee record”** — You signed in with a different Google account than the email on file.
- **“Use a Google account with a verified email address”** — Use a verified Google account.

## Password fallback

Password login is available when your account supports it:

1. On the login page, open the **password / admin fallback** section.
2. Enter the email and password on your account.
3. Sign in.

New employees may receive an **invitation** link to set a password. If email delivery is not configured, Admins may rely on Google Sign-In only—check with your lead.

You can change your password later from the account menu → **Change password**.

## After you sign in

- Your name and role appear in the header account menu.
- The sidebar shows modules your role can access.
- Open **Hub Handbook** under Overview anytime for training material.

## Privacy

The public **Privacy Policy** link on the login page describes how account data is handled. Use only your assigned account; do not share credentials.
