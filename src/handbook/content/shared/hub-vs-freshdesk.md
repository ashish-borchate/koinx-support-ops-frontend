KoinX uses two systems that work together. Confusing them is a common onboarding mistake—this page keeps the boundary clear.

## Freshdesk — customer support

**Freshdesk** is the system of record for:

- Live customer tickets and replies
- Ticket status, priority, and ownership
- Customer-facing SLAs and escalations on those tickets
- Survey responses tied to tickets (which may feed KPI data you later enter in the Hub)

When a customer is waiting on help, you work in **Freshdesk**. If a ticket must be escalated, that escalation happens in **Freshdesk**, not in the Support Hub.

## Support Hub — internal operations

The **Support Hub** is the system of record for:

- Agent and team **KPI records** (entered and managed by Supervisors, with Admin access as well)
- **QA evaluations**, appeals, and coaching follow-up
- **Documentation workflows** (Product Guides, Integration Steps, and related trackers)
- **Internal** issue reports, Support Updates, and personalized **Needs your attention** queues

When you need to fix or improve how we work as a team—or document something for colleagues—use the **Hub**.

## Quick decision guide

| Situation | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Reply to a customer on an open ticket | Freshdesk |
| Escalate a customer issue per process | Freshdesk |
| Enter this week’s SLA/CSAT numbers for an agent | Hub → KPI Performance |
| Review an agent’s closed ticket for QA | Hub → Quality Assurance |
| Flag that a published guide is wrong | Hub → Report issue on the guide (or Reported Issues) |
| Announce a process change to the team | Hub → Support Updates |

## Practical example

An agent closes a ticket in Freshdesk with a CSAT survey. Later that week, a **Supervisor** records the agent’s aggregated metrics in **KPI Performance**. A **Senior Agent** may score the same conversation in **QA**. The customer never sees the Hub; they only see Freshdesk.

## Retired Hub modules

Older Hub features such as a separate **Tasks** board or **Hub Escalations** are retired. If you see old links, they redirect to the dashboard or Reported Issues. Do not recreate ticket escalation state in the Hub.
