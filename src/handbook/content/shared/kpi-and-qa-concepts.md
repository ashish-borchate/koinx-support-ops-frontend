## KPI Performance

Supervisors are the **primary owners** of entering and updating agent KPI records. Admins can also edit and configure targets.

Metrics include first response time (FRT), average response time (ART), resolution time, SLA %, CSAT %, and QA contribution to a **composite scorecard** when scoring is configured. Data is entered **manually** in the Hub today.

Agents with access may see team aggregates on KPI Performance by product policy; confidential QA **evaluation detail** is still restricted on QA pages.

## Quality Assurance

- **QA subjects** are **Agents** only (not Senior Agents or Tech Agents as scored subjects).
- **Senior Agents** create and edit Agent QA reviews using the KoinX rubric.
- **Supervisor validation** on Agent QA records is **retired**—do not look for that workflow.

## Appeals

Agents may appeal scores on the current rubric within the **appeal window**. Code **defaults**: **7 days** to appeal, **3 days** to escalate after a reviewer decision. **Production values are unverified**—confirm with Admin if needed.

Appeal-adjusted scores may appear as the **effective QA score** in KPI-related views.

## Random QA audits

Supervisors periodically review **Agent QA evaluations** completed by Senior Agents to check scoring accuracy and consistency.

- Open an evaluation from **Quality Assurance** and complete a **Random QA audit** (or use **QA → Random audits** for history).
- Outcomes: **Accurate**, **Minor Issue Found**, or **Major Issue Found**.
- **Accurate** completes with no Senior Agent acknowledgement.
- **Minor** or **Major** requires Supervisor feedback; the **original QA reviewer** must **Acknowledge** or **Disagree** (dashboard tasks and notifications).
- Random QA audits are for **reviewer quality improvement**. They **do not change** the Agent’s QA score, criteria, or KPI.
- Agents challenge their own evaluation through the **QA appeal** process within the appeal window. A random audit does **not** extend or reset appeal or escalation deadlines.
