The Support Hub uses a **left sidebar** on desktop and a **menu sheet** on smaller screens. Your available links depend on your role permissions.

## Sidebar groups

| Group | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Dashboard, Hub Handbook, Management Overview, Support Updates, Reported Issues |
| Quality & performance | KPI Performance, Performance Center, Shift Calendar, Quality Assurance, Negative Reviews, Coaching |
| Knowledge & product | Product Guides, Integration Steps, Supported Files, and more |

Collapse a group by clicking its header; your preference is saved in the browser.

## Account menu (top right)

- **Profile** and **Change password**
- **User management** (Supervisors/Admins with access)
- **View as** (read-only preview of another employee's Hub)
- **Theme** (light / dark)
- **Log out**

## Hub Handbook

Open **Hub Handbook** under Overview for training, playbooks, walkthroughs, and search.

## View As

When View As is active, a banner appears and **you cannot save changes** in the Hub. Use it to understand another role's view, not to perform work on their behalf.

> **Tip:** If you cannot see a module a colleague mentions, you may lack permission—or they may have a different **operational** duty, not just broader access.
