## Reported Issues

Use Reported Issues for **internal** problems:

- **Resource-linked** reports (from Product Guides, Integration Steps, Knowledge Base)
- **General operations** reports (process, tooling, assignments)

Statuses include **Pending Review**, **In Progress**, **Resolved**, and **No Action Required** (with reason).

Supervisors triage resource-linked **Pending Review** items. Assignees work items assigned to them. @mentions notify colleagues.

Reported Issues do **not** replace Freshdesk tickets or auto-change guide status.

## Support Updates

Internal announcements with optional **acknowledgement**. Supervisors and Admins can manage posts; Senior Agents may create updates per permissions.

Check the dashboard feed for required acknowledgements when you start your shift.
