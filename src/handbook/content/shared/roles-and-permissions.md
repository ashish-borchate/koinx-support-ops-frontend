The Hub uses five roles: **Admin**, **Supervisor**, **Senior Agent**, **Agent**, and **Tech Agent**.

## Permissions vs responsibilities

| Idea | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Permission** | What the application allows (buttons, pages, APIs). |
| **Responsibility** | What your job expects you to do, which may be narrower or broader than permissions. |

Examples:

- **Supervisors** are responsible for **KPI data entry** (`kpi.edit`). Agents generally cannot edit team KPI.
- **Senior Agents** (e.g. Harsh) perform **Agent QA evaluations** (`qa.create`). Daily **DSAT** is done only by the **KPI reporting owner** configured by Admin—not every Senior Agent automatically.
- **Tech Agents** acknowledge **token migrations** and complete integration **frontend** tasks.

## Attention vs access

You may open a module but not receive every open item on your dashboard. Attention follows **assignment and role rules**, not “see all records.”

## View As

Leads may preview another user's Hub read-only. It does not change permissions in the database.
