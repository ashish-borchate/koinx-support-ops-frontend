The **Dashboard** (`/`) is your home screen. What you see depends on your **role** and **permissions**, not on personal preference settings.

## Two dashboard experiences

### Classic dashboard (typical for Agent and Tech Agent)

You will usually see:

- A short **greeting**
- **Needs your attention** — items assigned to you or owned by your role
- **Your pending tasks** — module task boards when you have open workflow items
- **Support Updates** — recent internal announcements (and acknowledgements when required)
- **Your week** KPI summary — for Agents with access to their own KPI view

### Operations Workspace (Admin, Supervisor, Senior Agent)

If your role uses the operations workspace, the dashboard adds leadership-oriented sections such as **Your responsibilities** (reference text), **Quick access** links, and for Supervisors additional team progress context. The same attention and module task concepts apply—only the layout and extra panels differ.

## Needs your attention vs module tasks

- **Needs your attention** highlights a focused set of actionable signals (for example, something you must publish, acknowledge, or triage).
- **Module tasks** group open work by module and stage (Product Guides, Integration Steps, Daily DSAT, and others).

Seeing a module in the sidebar does **not** mean every open item in that module appears on your dashboard—only items where you are the responsible actor.

## Management Overview

**Management Overview** is a separate page for leads (Supervisor, Senior Agent, Admin) with period KPI scorecards and operational rollups. It is not the same as the day-to-day dashboard, but complements it for reviews.

## View As (read-only)

Some roles can use **View As** from the account menu to see another employee’s Hub experience. While View As is active, a banner appears and **changes are blocked**—you are browsing read-only.

## Daily habits

1. Start on the **Dashboard** and clear or progress **attention** items.
2. Check **Support Updates** for anything requiring acknowledgement.
3. Use the sidebar to jump into the module you are working on.

Role-specific routines are in your **playbook** under Handbook → My role playbook.
