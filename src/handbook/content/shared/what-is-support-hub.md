The **KoinX Support Hub** (Support Operations in the app) is your team’s internal workspace for quality, performance, documentation, and operational tracking. It is where we record KPIs, run QA, maintain product guides and integration steps, log coaching, and coordinate internal issues—not where customers open tickets.

## What the Hub helps you do

- **Performance:** Enter and review weekly KPI metrics (response times, SLA, CSAT, and how QA contributes to scoring).
- **Quality:** Complete structured QA on agent tickets, manage appeals, and track coaching.
- **Knowledge:** Work through Product Guides, Integration Steps, Supported Files, Knowledge Base articles, and related trackers.
- **Operations:** Report internal issues, read Support Updates, and see what needs your attention on the dashboard.

## What the Hub is not

- **Not a ticketing system.** Customer conversations, ownership, and escalations stay in **Freshdesk**.
- **Not Excel.** The Hub is the system of record for the operational data we choose to track here.
- **Not automatic for every metric.** KPI entry is a deliberate team process—Supervisors (and Admins) maintain agent metrics in the Hub.

## How roles use the Hub

Everyone signs in to the same application, but your **role** changes which modules you see and what you are responsible for day to day. Your **permissions** control what the app allows; your **job responsibilities** may include tasks that only apply to certain people (for example, only the designated reporting owner completes Daily DSAT checks).

Use **Hub Handbook** for shared concepts, then open **your role playbook** for routines that match your job.
