## Purpose
Track daily DSAT monitoring accountability and negative review follow-up without mixing them into customer tickets.

## When to use it
You are a Senior Agent running the daily check, coaching on negative reviews, or helping someone understand who owns each step.

## Who is responsible
| Stage | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Daily DSAT Deep Dive | **Senior Agent** (any active employee in that role) |
| DSAT launch schedule | Admin sets IST launch date in KPI settings |
| Negative review records | Supervisor / Senior |
| Agent acknowledgement | Agent on own negative review records |

Daily DSAT Deep Dive is handled by the Senior Agent. Checks are completed Monday through Saturday, and any missed reporting days remain pending until completed.

## What requires follow-up
- When DSAT is found, continue in the Negative Reviews tracker — the daily check alone does not replace case work.
- Coaching and Freshdesk customer work stay outside this daily acknowledgement.

## What to check
- Open the dashboard **Daily DSAT Check** task or `/daily-dsat?date=YYYY-MM-DD` for each pending IST working day.
- Only one completion is recorded per calendar day for the team — coordinate if several Senior Agents are active.
