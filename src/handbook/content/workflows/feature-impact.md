## Purpose
Track product rollout progress across linked Product Guides.

## When to use it
You review Supervisor rollout health on Feature Impact dashboard.

## Who is responsible
Supervisor / Admin manage; status computed from linked guide publish progress.

## What happens next
Needs Action → Working → Complete based on % published.

## What requires follow-up
- Publishing guides still happens in Product Guides module.

## Common mistakes
- Marking impact Complete while linked guides remain unpublished.
