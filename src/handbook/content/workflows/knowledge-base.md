## Purpose
Host long-form internal HTML articles separate from Product Guides.

## When to use it
You distinguish KB articles from Product Guides when training or triaging issues.

## Who is responsible
Editors with manage access maintain Active / Needs Update / Archived.

## What happens next
No dashboard attention queue — check tracker directly. Report issue available on articles.

## What requires follow-up
- KB fixes are manual edits after Reported Issues.

## Common mistakes
- Expecting KB attention notifications (none configured).
