## Purpose
Capture product observations from the support floor in one tracker with a clear approve/reject decision.

## When to use it
You explain to the team what happens after submission or audit whether Supervisors are clearing the review queue.

## Who is responsible
| Stage | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Submit | Agents, Seniors, others with submit access |
| Pending Review attention | **Supervisor** role dashboard |
| Approve / Reject | Supervisor (Admin can also review) |

## What requires follow-up
- **No engineering ticket, guide change, or email** is sent automatically — operational follow-up is manual.
- Submitters see status in the tracker; there is no separate notifier in Hub v1.

## Before you approve
- You are recording a Supervisor decision in the tracker — not triggering product delivery.
- Reject with a reason the submitter can learn from; approve only when the observation is clear enough to act on offline.

## Before you submit
- One issue per submission when possible; vague “product is bad” items slow the review queue.
