## Purpose
Give Agents structured quality feedback on ticket work and a fair way to dispute rubric scores without using Freshdesk.

## When to use it
You are overseeing quality policy, supporting a dispute, or training Seniors — not creating every review yourself.

## Who is responsible
| Stage | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Create QA review | Senior Agent |
| View / acknowledge | Agent (subject) |
| Appeal | Agent within appeal window |
| Review appeal | Assigned reviewer (usually Senior Agent) |
| Escalation | Supervisor |
| Random QA audit (reviewer quality) | Supervisor |
| Acknowledge / disagree with random audit | Senior Agent who performed the original QA |
| Configure windows | Admin (read-only in handbook; live values from Hub settings) |

Supervisor validation on Agent QA is **not** part of the live workflow.

## Random QA audits (Supervisor → Senior Agent)

Supervisors periodically audit **Agent QA** evaluations that Senior Agents scored. This checks **reviewer** accuracy and consistency—not the Agent’s ticket work in isolation.

- **Accurate:** audit completes; no acknowledgement.
- **Minor / Major issue:** Supervisor feedback is required; the **original QA reviewer** must **Acknowledge** or **Disagree**.
- Random audits **do not** change criterion scores, effective QA score, or KPI. They **do not** open, extend, or reset Agent **appeal** or **escalation** windows.
- Agents who disagree with their **own** score still use the **appeal** workflow within the appeal window.

## Deadlines
- Agents have **4 calendar days** from QA evaluation creation to submit an eligible appeal.
- Eligible escalations must be submitted within **2 calendar days** of the reviewer’s appeal decision.
- The assigned reviewer handles the initial appeal; the Supervisor handles escalated appeals.
- Any approved score adjustment affects the **effective QA score** used in KPI-related views.

## What requires follow-up
- Coaching or 1:1s after poor scores — use Coaching Center manually.
- Customer impact — still handled in **Freshdesk**, not by closing a QA record.

## Before you submit (appeal)
- Note the appeal deadline on the review (Admins see live windows in KPI/QA settings and on this handbook page when signed in as Admin).
- Tie each challenged criterion to a specific ticket moment or policy — vague appeals are hard to decide fairly.
- Attach or reference evidence the reviewer can verify without guessing.

## If something goes wrong
- **Missed appeal window:** The Hub blocks late appeals; escalate through your Supervisor for operational exceptions, not by editing the record.
- **Disagree with escalation outcome:** Document coaching follow-up; the effective score after closure is what KPI views use.
