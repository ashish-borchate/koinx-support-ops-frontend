## Purpose
Broadcast internal announcements with optional acknowledgement.

## When to use it
You publish policy changes or verify ack requirements after editing a post.

## Who is responsible
Create: Senior Agent, Supervisor, Admin. Manage/pin: Supervisor, Admin.

## What happens next
Employees read from dashboard feed; material edits may reset acknowledgement version.

## What requires follow-up
- Support Updates are **not** in the global Needs your attention aggregator — rely on dashboard feed.

## Common mistakes
- Assuming everyone was notified by attention cards alone.
