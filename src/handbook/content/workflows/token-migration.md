## Purpose
Record coin migrations and confirm Tech has acknowledged informed work.

## When to use it
You verify tech ack backlog on the dashboard.

## Who is responsible
Record: Supervisor, Senior, Admin. Ack: Tech Agent.

## What happens next
When `informedTech` is set and ack pending, Tech sees attention until acknowledged.

## What requires follow-up
- Manual coordination if Tech is out of office — reassign or defer in tracker.

## Common mistakes
- Tech Agent trying to publish integrations instead of acking migration tasks.
